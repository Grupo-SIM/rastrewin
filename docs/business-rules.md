# Business Rules

Source of truth for product and domain rules. Keep engineering workflow and agent rules in `AGENTS.md`.

## Products

- **Rastrewin raffles** (`frontend`, `backend`): landing, sign-up and checkout; `/dashboard` with the user's raffles; `/admin` where the admin defines each raffle's winner. A winner is shown to the user only when the raffle time is over **and** the admin has set one; otherwise the UI shows "Apurando resultado...". Raffle data is mocked.
- **Tracking** (`track`): server-side tracking that captures visitors and events, sends them to Meta Conversions API and GA4, receives sales relayed by the rastrewin backend and shows everything in a panel. Its rules (RLS, secrets, retention, geolocation attribution) live in [`track/AGENTS.md`](../track/AGENTS.md) and [`track/SECURITY.md`](../track/SECURITY.md).
- **VSL page** (`vsl`): static video sales letter page that precedes https://rastrewin.com, served at vsl.rastrewin.com. No backend and no database; it loads the track script (`t.js`) to record the visitor and the ad origin. Rules live in [`vsl/AGENTS.md`](../vsl/AGENTS.md).

## Sales and attribution

- Funnel: ad → VSL → rastrewin.com sign-up form → SimIntPay checkout (a payment link per plan).
- Both sites load `https://track.rastrewin.com/t.js`. Visits from the Meta and Google networks (ad review and link-preview robots that run the page) are ignored by the track: they are not visitors and nothing is sent to Meta/GA4 for them. The visitor id cookie (`_trck`) is set on `.rastrewin.com`, so the VSL and rastrewin.com share the visitor.
- On submit, the form identifies the lead and sends `InitiateCheckout` to the track. It also sends the visitor's tracking (`src` = `trck_user_id`, UTMs, `fbc`, `fbp`) with the sign-up; the backend stores it.
- SimIntPay sends each sale event to the backend, which confirms the purchase and relays the event to the track. The track ties the sale to the visit through `src`, falling back to e-mail or phone. Details: [SimIntPay integration](development/simintpay.md).
- Only the first purchase goes to Meta and GA4 as `Purchase`. Repurchases and renewals (the buyer already had a paid order) are not sent.
- **Renewals in the panel (one rule for every tab):** revenue counts everything, because it is money that came in; acquisition numbers count only new sales. So Receita (Visão geral and Faturamento) and Faturamento's Compras/Ticket include renewals, and the panel shows how much came from them (Visão geral hint, Faturamento "Renovações" card). Visão geral's Compras, Conversão, funnel and ROAS, plus Geo, Campanhas, Páginas and Vídeos, count only new sales.
- Refund and chargeback revoke the plan of the order that was refunded. The track marks the sale refunded and sends a GA4 `refund`; Meta receives nothing for refunds.
- Once a sale is approved, its value, product, UTMs and visitor link are fixed. Later events for the same order (refund requested, refund, chargeback) change only the status and the refund date, so the amount they carry never rewrites revenue and the tracking the backend attaches to them never moves the sale to another campaign. Only a new approval of the same order can update those fields. Each raw event stays in `webhook_events`.
- Every track event carries the same UTMs as `identify`, so an event that reaches the server before `identify` (the first PageView) still records its origin.

## Panel analytics (track)

- **Periods and comparison** (panel time zone): the presets follow Meta Ads Manager. "Últimos N dias" are the N full days before today (they end yesterday, like Ads Manager, so the same filter shows the same spend in both places); "Hoje", "Esta semana" and "Este mês" include today. Comparison ("vs anterior"): "Este mês" compares with the same days of the previous month (1–8 Nov × 1–8 Oct; if the previous month is shorter, it stops at its last day); "Mês passado" compares with the whole month before it; every other preset and custom ranges compare with the same number of days right before; "Máximo" does not compare. A KPI shows "novo" only when the previous value was zero and the current one is not, and "sem comparação" when either side does not exist (e.g. conversion without visitors, average ticket without sales, which then shows "—").
- **Meta spend when the detail is cut:** the ad-level insights stop at 10 pages (5,000 ad × day rows). When Meta still had more rows, the track fetches the account totals (one small account-level call) and uses them for spend, ROAS, CPA and the daily chart in Campanhas and Visão geral; the campaign tree stays partial and the panel shows a warning. If even the account totals fail, the warning says the spend shown is below the real one.
- **Campaign status and budget (Campanhas):** each campaign, ad set and ad shows its Meta delivery status (Ativa, Pausada, Em análise, Reprovada, Com problema, Pagamento pendente, Arquivada; Concluída once its end date has passed) with icon and label, and its daily or lifetime budget (with what remains) at the level where it was set: the campaign when it uses campaign budget, otherwise each ad set. Active campaigns with no spend in the period are listed at the end with zero metrics and "sem entrega no período"; paused, archived or finished campaigns without spend are not listed. Campaigns with spend in the period that Meta reports as archived, or no longer lists at all (deleted), go into a collapsed "Arquivadas ou excluídas" group at the end, showing their summed numbers; their spend stays in every total. A campaign only counts as deleted when that ad account's list came back complete, so a missing or cut list never hides an active campaign. Status and budget come from one cached copy per ad account, refreshed at most hourly (and on "Atualizar agora") inside the same rate limit; they never change spend, ROAS or attribution. If Meta does not answer, the tree shows no status and the panel says so.
- **Visão geral:** "Gasto" is the Meta Ads spend of all active ad accounts, read through the same cache and rate limit as Campanhas (no extra Meta call when either tab already loaded the period). "ROAS" here is the overall ROAS: revenue of the period's new sales ÷ spend (renewals excluded), including new sales that did not come from ads; the campaign-attributed ROAS (UTM) stays in Campanhas.
- **Páginas:** conversion per page, where a page is the domain + path without query string (UTMs, `fbclid`) or trailing slash. A person counts on **every** page they viewed in the period, so a buyer who saw the VSL and the sign-up page counts on both and the rows do not add up to the total. Checkout = viewers who fired `InitiateCheckout` in the period; purchases = viewers' approved sales in the period, renewals excluded (same rules as Faturamento). The table pages through every page in steps of 100 (ordered by users); the KPI cards always cover all pages.
- **Eventos:** each row shows the visitor ID, which opens that visitor's journey directly. The event type filter accepts several types at once (`?tipo=PageView,Lead`; a link with one type still works) and applies when the menu closes.
- **Geo:** the countries chart shows the top 10 plus "Outros / desconhecido" and its table view lists every country. The cities list shows 20 at a time with "Ver mais", up to the 500 largest, and states the period's total number of cities.
- **Vídeos:** retention of the VSL video. The VSL reports each play's progress (`trck('video')`): furthest point reached, seconds really watched (skips and replays don't count), the end and the second the button unlocks. One row per play in `video_views`. Play rate = plays ÷ PageViews of the video's page, counting PageViews only from the video's first recorded play (before it, plays could not be measured, so older views would lower the rate); the ⓘ shows that start when it falls inside the period. How to read the tab and why it is calculated this way: [track/docs/aba-videos.md](../track/docs/aba-videos.md). This data stays in the panel: it never goes to Meta or GA4 and is not an event (`events_log`, the overview's "Eventos" KPI and the funnel are unchanged).
- **Visitor journey** (`/painel/visitante/<trck_user_id>`, opened from the Eventos visitor column and the event and purchase details): everything recorded about one visitor, with no period filter. Shows visitor data (e-mail; name and phone only from a sale event such as a generated PIX or a purchase, since sign-up stores them hashed), origin and attribution (first touch = oldest record with UTMs, last touch = the visitor's current UTMs, first page, referrer, Meta ad click from `_fbc`), each purchase with how it was linked and its campaign › ad set › ad from the UTMs, other visitors with the same e-mail or phone hash (other devices), and a chronological timeline of events, video plays and every sale event (PIX generated, approved, refunded…) with the UTMs, page, location, IP and device of each record. Shows the newest 200 records first, with "load earlier".
- **Bot visits are ignored** (no visitor, no event, nothing sent to Meta/GA4): Meta and Google crawler networks, cloud and hosting networks (AWS, Azure, Google Cloud, Alibaba, Tencent, Oracle, DigitalOcean, OVH, Hetzner…), browsers that declare themselves bots, plain Chrome below version 100 (Chrome auto-updates; manufacturer and in-app browsers that ship older engines are not affected) and browsers driven by automation (`navigator.webdriver`). iCloud Private Relay networks are never blocked. Configurações › Saúde counts the ignored visits by reason since the last restart. Note for local tests: Playwright sets `navigator.webdriver`, so pages it opens are ignored unless it runs with `--disable-blink-features=AutomationControlled`.
- **System health** (Configurações › Saúde, and a banner on every panel page when something needs attention): database size (sum of all tables) and growth estimated from the last 7 days, with a warning above 5 GB; the daily cleanup (late after 36 h without running); the backend's sale relay queue (heartbeat every minute; warns when a sale waits more than 30 min, the backend gave up on one, or there is no heartbeat for 10 min; "Tentar de novo" requeues the given-up sales); new sales from the last 7 days that did not reach Meta or GA4 after the 5 automatic attempts.
- **Daily summary tables: deliberately not built.** The heaviest panel numbers are unique users, which cannot be added up day by day (one person on three days would count three times), so pre-aggregated daily rows would barely speed them up while adding a table, a nightly job and a second source for the same number. Revisit when Configurações › Saúde shows `events_log` near a few million rows or a panel page takes more than 2 s.
- **Daily cleanup does not change the numbers:** it only empties the four technical JSON payloads of `events_log` rows older than 14 days (what was sent to and received from Meta/GA4). Event rows, URLs, visitors, purchases and video plays are kept, so KPIs, funnel, Páginas, Vídeos, campaigns/ROAS and the sale → visitor link are unaffected. After 14 days the event detail shows empty JSON tabs and Meta status "expirado".
